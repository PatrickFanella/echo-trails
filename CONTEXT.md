# Context Glossary

## EchoTrails

EchoTrails is a public place-stories product: people leave short echoes tied to real-world places so others can discover the stories of those places.

## Echo

An Echo is a short piece of user-created content attached to a real-world place. Every Echo has a required short title. Echoes may be text, audio, photo, video, or a 1-5 photo set. An Echo uses one content mode at a time, with optional caption text for media modes.

## Stitch

A Stitch is an Echo that is linked as a response to another Echo while remaining its own Echo at its own Place. A Stitch must be created within the allowed boundary of its Place.

## Reply

A Reply is a text-only comment in the conversation thread under an Echo. Unlike a Stitch, a Reply is not its own Place-linked Echo, does not appear independently in Place discovery, and may be created from anywhere. Replies do not have titles.

## Place

A Place is a named real-world point, venue, landmark, address, or area with coordinates and a human-readable name. A Place is not an arbitrary unnamed map pixel. Places normally come from a canonical place source; users may request a new Place when no suitable match exists.

## Place Subscription

A Place Subscription means a User has asked to receive updates about activity at a Place. EchoTrails supports Place Subscriptions without requiring Users to follow other Users in the first version.

## Public Echo Location

A Public Echo Location is the approximate map position shown to other users for an Echo. It may be jittered from the exact creation coordinate to protect user safety.

## User

A User is an account holder who can create Echoes. Users may use pseudonymous public identities, but public Echoes are not truly anonymous in the first version. Every public Echo is attributed to a stable public handle.
